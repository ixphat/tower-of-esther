# 01 Lore

The tower's top holds an Empty Throne. Its creator, Esther, chose to die and left the seat empty, its master Ezra is rebuilding a god from the memories of fallen climbers with the help of a quiet cult, and the Watchers want the seat for themselves. Still adjustable.

## Truth of the Tower (direction decided: "lifeboat of dying worlds")

- Every floor is a separate world: its own universe, genre, rules and people. They are not parts of one planet.
- Each world was about to vanish. The Tower took it in before it disappeared and keeps it as a floor.
- Floor masters are the last survivors of their worlds, who stayed to guard what is left.
- The creator was among the last survivors of the first world and built the Tower so worlds would not vanish. The first person they saved from that world was Ezra. The creator's true name is **Esther**. The system never uses it and only says "the Creator"; the name appears in the creator's will in Arc 5.
- At first the Tower ran on the creator's own memories. When the creator saw that the Tower would have to consume others to keep going, they stopped feeding it and chose to die. They left the Throne empty on purpose, so someone else would decide whether to keep or release the worlds, because they could not choose themselves.
- After the creator's death the Tower began taking memories from climbers who fail. Climbers lose the memories and bonds they made inside the Tower, and those memories become the fuel that sustains the worlds. Arc 3 reveals this.
- The creator left a will and clues scattered across the floors as secrets and hidden events, for those who ask and search. The Tower Master does not know about them, or refuses to accept them.
- The Tower Master has been diverting climbers' fuel to assemble a new creator out of the memories of fallen climbers (see below).
- The Empty Throne is the authority to decide the fate of every world in the Tower. The Tower Master keeps it empty, waiting for the creator to return.

### Endings (draft)

| Ending | Choice | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep | Take the Throne and keep all worlds | Every floor survives, but the Tower keeps consuming climbers |
| Release | Let the worlds end | Every world rests, but floor masters and everything on the floors disappear |
| Third way (secret) | Find a way to sustain the worlds without consuming climbers | Needs items and knowledge gathered across all five arcs |
| Let the revival finish | Allow the Tower Master's work to complete | The assembled creator takes the Throne. It is built from every climber's lost memories, and decides the fate of the worlds with that combined will. Outcome uncertain (draft) |

## The Tower Master, his cult and the Watchers (structure, roster and names decided, details draft)

### Tower Master (floor 50): Ezra

| Element | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Who | Ezra, the creator's most devoted servant, who worships the creator as the god of all things. Master of the last floor |
| History | The first survivor the creator ever saved, taken from the creator's own first world (floor 1), so he believes his whole life belongs to the creator |
| Manner | Polite, gentle, never angry. His faith is sincere, which makes him more frightening than cruelty would: he does terrible things out of love |
| Belief | The god will descend again. Until then he keeps the Throne empty. Anyone who comes to take it is a blasphemer |
| Secret work | He tampers with floors across the Tower and gathers the memories climbers lose, to rebuild the creator, with the help of his cult. These are the first signs the protagonist notices in Arc 1 |
| What he does not face | He believes the one who returns will be the true creator. In fact it is assembled from the lives of other people. He also refuses to accept that the creator left by choice |
| Tragedy | He waits and works for a god who chose to leave, and spends the lives of the people he guards to do it |

### The Order of the Descent (his cult, working name)

| Element | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Who | Followers of the creator who wait for the god's descent. Members include climbers who lost memories and found purpose, survivors of absorbed worlds, and a few who joined freely. Most do not know what the Order's work costs |
| Belief | The god will come down. The Order tells those who lost memories that the descent will give back what they lost |
| Sign | A white empty chair, the Empty Throne drawn simply. Phrase: "He will come down" |
| Manner | Small, polite and helpful. They look benign. They pray for the fallen, offer comfort and give small gifts |
| Work | Place marks on floors, feed or steer the tampering, quietly make climbers more likely to fail, and gather the memories the Tower takes. Ezra leads them from floor 50 |
| Presence | A small hint on every floor, growing across the arcs. In Arc 1 the climber sees only the sign and polite strangers; the link to Ezra is revealed in Arc 4 |

### Watchers (floors 47, 48, 49)

| Element | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Who | Lesser gods and other beings. Includes former climbers who reached the top but could not pass floor 50 |
| Where | Sealed on floors 47, 48 and 49. They cannot come down or act directly. They first appear to the climber as projections on floor 9 |
| Stance | They challenge the creator's authority and the Tower Master, and want the Throne for themselves. They hold the Order in contempt |
| How they interfere | Watch climbers like a livestream, tip coins for exciting play, give sudden quests, and send hostile interference |
| Reach downward | In Arc 4 they contest the floors below through climbers and floor rules. Their homes stay on 47–49 |

## Protagonist (decided)

The player creates their own climber (name and class are the player's). The climber wakes up inside the Tower with no memory of anything before it, so there is no outside world or origin to write. Every player's climber shares one fixed motive, so the story fits all of them without needing many versions.

| Element | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Who | A climber with no past. Name, looks and personality are left blank for the player |
| Motive | Not glory, money or rescue. They want to learn the truth: what the Tower is for, what it really does, and why they are here |
| The spark | They open their eyes inside the Tower. The system tells them only that they are a climber and must climb. Most accept this. The protagonist asks: why? |
| Personality frame | Curious, stubborn, never takes a system message at face value. Free to play any tone on top of that |
| Why it matters | A climber who keeps asking questions is rare, so the Watchers take special notice in Arc 2. The Tower's secrets answer only those who look, including the creator's will and clues left on the floors |
| Defeat | Each defeat takes memories made inside the Tower (people met, things learned, places seen). The question "why am I here" is the last thing the climber holds onto |
| Other climbers | Real players and NPCs have their own reasons, formed inside the Tower: glory, survival, someone they met here. The protagonist is the one who keeps asking why |

### Truth arc

The protagonist's motive is answered in steps, so the player's journey is the reveal of the Tower.

| Arc | What the protagonist learns |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The first signs that floors are being tampered with; the system messages leave things out; the first clues left by the creator; a white empty-chair mark and polite strangers who say "He will come down"; the Watchers exist and are watching (floor 9) |
| 2 | One or more Watchers take special interest in this climber; the Order of the Descent appears openly and recruits climbers |
| 3 | What the Tower takes from those who fail, and where it goes: into a new creator being assembled |
| 4 | Who is tampering with the floors and why; the Order serves Ezra; the Watchers want the Throne; the need to choose a side |
| 5 | The creator Esther chose to die, Ezra's faith, what the assembled creator really is, and the choice over all the worlds |

## World Setting

Draft. A single Tower that every climber, and every real player, climbs together. Climbers know of no world outside it.

| Element | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Climbers | People who wake inside the Tower with no memory of before. They are survivors of worlds that could not be saved whole, and their memories were spent when the Tower pulled them in. Floor 2 (Rest Town) becomes home |
| System | Climbers get status windows, grades and skills. The Tower speaks through system messages and grades every clear |
| Defeat | A defeated climber is returned to the last safe point and loses memories made inside the Tower, which in play is lost EXP progress and a lost bond level (see 04 Systems). These memories become fuel for the worlds, and part is diverted to Ezra's revival. Arc 3 reveals it |
| Floors | Each floor is a different world the Tower took in before it vanished. Genre floors (murim, palace, zombie city) are those worlds' remains; floor masters are their last survivors |
| Tower Master | Ezra rules floor 50 and keeps the Throne empty (see above) |
| Order of the Descent | Ezra's cult, with a small hint on every floor (see above) |
| Watchers | Sealed on floors 47–49 (see above) |
| Other climbers | Are the real players (Option B). Their first clears, messages and rankings are part of the world |
| Tone | Game-like system on the surface, real tragedy underneath |

## Core story

- The tower's creator, Esther, chose to die and left the Empty Throne for someone else to fill. Whoever takes it decides the fate of all worlds.
- The **Tower Master**, Ezra, keeps the Throne empty for the creator's return and secretly tampers with floors to rebuild the creator from the memories fallen climbers lose. His cult, the **Order of the Descent**, helps him.
- The **Watchers** are lesser gods and beings, including former climbers who could not pass floor 50. They are sealed on floors 47–49 and want the Throne.
- They can't act directly, so they watch climbers like a livestream and interfere, using climbers as pawns to claim the throne.
- Every interference in the game is motivated by this: they want the player, or want the player gone.

## Open questions

- What do Esther's will and clues say, and where on the floors are they found? Three clues are placed in Arc 1 (floors 1, 8 and 10). They must not point the player toward a single ending.
- What is the assembled creator like if the revival finishes (personality, how it decides)? Needed for the "Let the revival finish" ending.
- Is there any way out of the Tower other than the Throne?
- How many members of the Order know what its work costs, and does the Order have its own ranks and leaders besides Ezra?
- Does the Order's official name stay "Order of the Descent", or change?
