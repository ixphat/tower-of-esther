## Class master chain (the same for every class)

Every class master (floors 3 to 8) runs the same three-part chain, so no class has an easier path (the fairness rule in 04 Systems). The first part is the quest chain written in each floor's Masters row below. The other two are described here, with one condition per class.

| Part | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Teaching chain | Take the master's quest, pass a trial, deliver what is asked, then learn the class's three first skills (Novice). This is the chain in each floor's Masters row |
| 2. Stage-up trials | When one of the class's skills reaches Lv 10, the master sets a special fight with a condition tied to that skill. Passing it moves the skill to the Intermediate stage. There are three trials, one per skill, and each can be retried without limit with no penalty for failing. Arc 1 masters can test up to Intermediate |
| 3. Advancement quest | After clearing floor 10 (the climber holds the Wall Seal), the climber returns to the master, chooses one of the class's two branches, and passes one branch trial. The master then teaches the three branch skills, and the level cap rises to 45. Stat bonuses, signature traits and skills are in 04 Systems |

## Bondable NPCs

Draft. The bond levels and their general rewards are in 04 Systems (Bonds). This section lists, for every Arc 1 floor, which named NPCs can be bonded, what each likes, and what they give at Trusted and Close. In total 31 NPCs, one to four per floor.

Rules for the lists:

- Gifts: each NPC likes two kinds of gift (+10 points). Any other gift is +5. Nobody dislikes anything, so a wrong gift costs nothing.
- Trusted (4): the NPC's own short quest, a secret clue, and one rare crafting item. The quest is the part named after the arrow below.
- Close (5): a closing scene, then the climber chooses one reward: a small unique skill (scrap level, Novice, open to every class, no tag) or another rare crafting item. Both are listed.
- Rewards stay small and the same in weight across NPCs and floors, so no floor or class is favored. The skills are fusion ingredients first and combat help second.
- Not bondable: the Watchers, members of the Order (the robed visitor, the emissaries, the recruiter, the scribe), hidden masters (they teach through their quests) and enemies.
- Wandering masters are bondable like anyone else. Their bond level (Friendly, or Trusted for the Retired Heavenly Demon) is also the gate to their teaching, and the rewards below come on top of it.
- The Retired Heavenly Demon's Trusted quest is the teaching quest itself (floor 4), so his Trusted reward is the rare item for completing it.

Item counter: with the item from that floor, the rule is weakened by about half. Full counter: with that floor's item and skill together, the rule becomes a small advantage for the climber. The counter is read from what the climber carries and has learned, and the Wall is a spike that level alone cannot pass. Draft table (item, then full counter): floor 3 status reset: remedies and cure ingredients let good statuses survive one reset, and Mender status skills keep some buffs through resets. Floor 4 crit ×2: sect pills reduce the damage taken from crits, and the sect arts make the multiplier apply only to the climber. Floor 5 immortal night enemies: the house-warding talisman lets enemies die within a short radius, and spirit skills weaken night enemies greatly. Floor 6 reputation = HP: noble seals and court attire reduce reputation loss, and court skills remove the witness penalty. Floor 7 no healing: field medicine heals more, and survival skills restore a little HP after each fight. Floor 8 no map: elven materials craft a compass that works on this floor, and forest skills keep the notebook sketch from fading as fast. Floor 9 handicaps: gladiator gear reduces handicap penalties, and the risk skill adds extra rewards for handicaps taken. All numbers are TBD. Counters are the same for every class |
