# 02 Floor Design

Every floor is a world with its own rule, story and secrets, never just a dungeon with stronger monsters. Principles come from research on what tower-manhwa readers like.

## Principles

1. **One unique rule per floor** that changes how turn-based combat plays.
2. **Genre floors**: riffs on familiar genres (murim, villainess, zombie, time loop) plus Thai ghosts and folklore as a unique selling point.
3. **Floor masters have their own tragedy.** Floors can be cleared by more than killing: save, deceive, or fulfill their wish.
4. **Clear grade F–SSS**: the Tower grades each clear out of 100 points on four factors, with weights and conditions set per floor (see Clear grade below). Rewards scale with grade, giving a reason to replay old floors.
5. **Wall floor every 10 floors**: a big difficulty spike that level alone can't pass; needs items or skills gathered earlier. Leaderboards show where the server is stuck.
6. **Watchers** give coins for exciting play (winning near death, risky choices) and drop sudden quests with a cost attached.
7. **Real combat on every floor, shaped by the rule.** Each floor has its own enemies, an elite, a mini-boss and a floor master fight, all built around the floor rule. A floor whose rule removes combat is the rare exception. Peaceful paths are another hard route (a trial, a rite under attack, a puzzle), never a skip.
8. **A hint of the Order of the Descent on every floor.** The Tower Master's cult leaves a small sign on each floor (see 01 Lore). Hints escalate across the arcs: in Arc 1 they are only the sign and polite strangers, and the link to Ezra is revealed later.
9. **Monsters matter.** Fighting monsters gives EXP and drops that feed crafting, in the field and during quests. Farming is a normal way to play (see 04 Systems).
10. **Some floors hide a wandering master.** A skilled person who belongs to no class or weapon and teaches the climber who looks for them and earns their trust. Most are ordinary teachers of odd, weak skills; a rare few are fierce teachers whose strong skills are paid for with a hard quest (see Wandering masters in 04 Systems). Nothing on a floor requires them.

## Avoid

- Floors that are only monsters and leveling, with no story, rule or choices.
- Floors that are only fetch or talk quests with no combat challenge (unless the floor rule removes combat).
- Power scaling that breaks the world's own rules.
- A protagonist who can't lose.
- Overused tropes played straight with no twist.

## Clear grade

All numbers are drafts.

The Tower scores every clear out of 100 points on four factors. The weights and what counts differ per floor, so each floor's rule is reflected in its grade.

| Factor | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| Path | Which way the floor was cleared. A harder or more complete path earns more |
| Content | Quests and chapters done, bonds built (the bond levels reached with the floor's named NPCs, see 04 Systems), people saved |
| Secrets | Hidden events and clues found |
| Style | Something tied to the floor's rule or pace, such as turns used, cycles used, reputation kept, risk points taken, or exploration finds |

Points to grade:

| Points | Grade |
| --- | --- |
| 0–19 | F |
| 20–34 | E |
| 35–49 | D |
| 50–64 | C |
| 65–74 | B |
| 75–84 | A |
| 85–94 | S |
| 95–100 | SS |
| 95–100 plus the floor's hidden condition | SSS |

Every floor has one hidden SSS condition for players to discover. The criteria for each floor in Arc 1 are in 03 Arc 1.

Rewards scale with grade (draft): the first clear gives EXP and drops that rise with the grade (about ×1 at F up to ×3 at SSS; the EXP part is in 04 Systems). A clear of B or better also gives one guaranteed piece of that world's set, in addition to the set pieces that drop from the floor's elites, mini-bosses and floor master (see 04 Systems). A clear of S or better gives a clue toward a rare recipe. Only the best grade is kept, so replaying a floor can raise it.

## Floor template

| Part | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Story | Setting and the world's problem |
| NPCs | Key characters on the floor, and which of them can be bonded (see 04 Systems) |
| Floor master | Who rules the floor and their tragedy |
| Floor rule | The one rule that changes combat |
| Combat | Combat weight (none, light, medium, heavy); 3–5 enemy types shaped by the floor rule, including 1 elite; a mini-boss; where fights are; what they drop (materials and items that exist only in this world) |
| Floor master fight | The floor master as a real boss fight, and how each clear path changes it. Peaceful paths are another hard route, never a skip |
| Main quest | Linear chain; completing it opens the next floor |
| Side quests | Optional; give items, recipes or NPC relationships |
| Masters | Any weapon or class master on this floor, the quest chain to learn from them, and their stage-up trials (see 04 Systems and the class master chain in 03 Arc 1) |
| Hidden master | The master of an add-on hidden class on this floor, if any, and how the climber finds and learns from them (see 04 Systems) |
| Wandering master | A wandering master on this floor, if any: ordinary or fierce, how they are found, the bond level they need, their errand or quest, and the skills they teach (see 04 Systems) |
| Cult hint | A small sign of the Order of the Descent for this floor (a mark, a sermon, a polite stranger), escalating across the arcs |
| Hidden events | Unlocked by secret conditions (right place, right time, odd choices) |
| Exploration | Gathering spots, chests, secret paths |
| Floor-exclusive | Materials, skills or weapons that exist only in this world |
| Shop | Stock themed to the floor's story |
| Clear condition | Boss, trial, or a story decision |
| Clear grade | The points for each of the four factors on this floor and one hidden SSS condition (see Clear grade above) |
